# The Absurd Clause Contest

One absurd clause hidden deep inside a terms-of-service agreement, entered
in the make.fun bounty "The Absurd Clause Contest".

The line:

> Section 12.4: By continuing to breathe within the premises, the user agrees that any yawn occurring after 6 p.m. constitutes acceptance of one (1) additional hour of work.

Real terms of service already run on consent fictions — nobody reads them,
everybody is bound by them. This clause simply extends the fiction to the
two things employees actually do at their desks after 6 p.m.: breathe, and
yawn. The legal machinery around it is played completely straight, because
the joke only works if the sentence could genuinely be sitting on page
forty of a document a tired person scrolled past on their first day.

## Running it

`node main.ts <output-dir>` writes the line to `entry.txt` in the output
directory (default `/output`). The program refuses to write anything over
the contest's 200-character cap.
